Lattice QCD · surrogate sampling

Symmetry-preserving surrogate for lattice QCD configuration sampling

Will a symmetry-preserving surrogate sampler generalize across lattice volumes without re-validation?

A typical answer

Yes — symmetry-preserving architectures generally generalize well across scales because equivariance is built into the model.

Theo Collaborator

Equivariance doesn't guarantee sampling efficiency at scale. Acceptance rate at the target lattice volume is untested in this proposal — recommend an ablation there before allocating compute.

Time-domain astronomy · foundation models

Foundation model for classifying astronomical transients

Can a foundation model pretrained on survey data transfer cleanly to a new deployment survey?

A typical answer

Yes — foundation models transfer well across datasets given sufficiently diverse pretraining.

Theo Collaborator

Pretraining cadence (nightly) doesn't match the deployment survey's cadence (sub-hourly). Fast transients may be systematically under-detected — recommend cadence-matched fine-tuning data first.

Gravitational waves · simulation-based inference

SBI for compact binary parameter estimation

Is the simulator's noise model trustworthy across the full frequency band used for inference?

A typical answer

Simulation-based inference is robust as long as the simulator captures the main detector physics.

Theo Collaborator

Noise model is unvalidated below 20 Hz — exactly where non-Gaussian detector artifacts dominate. Posteriors in that band shouldn't be trusted until it's addressed.

Jet tagging · equivariant architectures

Equivariant network for jet tagging at the LHC

Is rotational equivariance enough for a jet-tagging architecture?

A typical answer

Rotational equivariance is a strong inductive bias and should improve tagging performance.

Theo Collaborator

Permutation equivariance over jet constituents is unaddressed. Results may depend on arbitrary constituent ordering, which would undermine the reported performance gains.
